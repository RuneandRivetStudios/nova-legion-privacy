Nova Legion

Privacy Policy | Rune and Rivet Studios

Effective date: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Rune and Rivet Studios respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Nova Legion, our Android game, handles information.

2. Information We Collect

Nova Legion does not require account registration and is not designed to collect personal information from players.

The game does not include advertising or analytics services. Rune and Rivet Studios does not operate a game server that receives player progress.

3. Game Progress and Local Storage

Nova Legion stores game progress, earned coins, unlocked equipment, and other gameplay preferences locally on the player's device.

This information is used to save and restore gameplay progress.

4. Android Backup and Restore

Depending on the player's device settings and Android backup configuration, eligible local game data may be backed up and restored through Android's system backup services.

Such backups are managed by the device's operating system and backup provider, not by a cloud-save service operated by Rune and Rivet Studios.

5. Advertising and Purchases

Nova Legion does not display advertisements and does not offer in-app purchases. Any initial purchase of the game is handled by the app store.

6. Children's Privacy

Nova Legion is intended for a broad audience, including children. The game does not require children to provide personal information, create accounts, or communicate with other players.

Rune and Rivet Studios is committed to following applicable children's privacy requirements.

7. Data Sharing

Rune and Rivet Studios does not sell player information or intentionally share player data with advertisers or analytics providers.

Android system backup and app-store transactions may involve separate services governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress is stored locally and can be removed through the game's reset functionality or by clearing the application's storage. Uninstalling the game generally removes local application data, although system-managed backups may remain subject to the backup provider's retention and deletion practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us
